- `issue-tracker.md` — **the contract**: what "publish to the issue tracker" means here (Trellis tasks).
- `triage-labels.md` — canonical triage roles → where they are stored (`meta.triage` / `Status:` lines).

- `hygiene.md` — cleanup-ticket acceptance contract: delete/reorder/rename/equivalent-refactor only, scope-prefix gate, split-plan-first for files over the line threshold; pairs with the `hygiene-sweep` skill.
